ABOUT

Three chapters, one journey.

I spent more than ten years in the banking sector — working with people, numbers and deadlines. It taught me discipline, patience and how to solve problems calmly when everything is on fire.

Today I run a computer shop in Cumilla. I sell, repair and support the machines people depend on every day. It is a different kind of work, but the same principle: be honest, explain clearly, and stand behind what you sell.

And then there is the bike. Long rides across Bangladesh keep me curious and keep everything else in perspective.
